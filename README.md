## 📥 Step-by-Step: Bringing Brave to Life on Windows

### 🧩 Step 1 — Prepare Your Environment
Before the installer runs, take thirty seconds to ensure your Windows environment is ready. Close other browsers. Make sure your drive has a few gigabytes of slack. Confirm your Windows Update is current. A clean canvas produces a cleaner painting.

### 🧩 Step 2 — Launch the Installer
Double-click the installer you retrieved. Windows may show a "User Account Control" prompt — this is normal for any application that touches your system directories. Confirm it, and let the installer proceed.

### 🧩 Step 3 — Choose Install Location
The default location on most systems is `C:\Program Files\BraveSoftware\`. You can accept this or redirect to another drive if you prefer to keep your system partition lean.

### 🧩 Step 4 — First Launch Walkthrough
On first launch, you'll be greeted by an import wizard. You may choose to migrate bookmarks, history, and passwords from another browser, or you may start with a pristine profile. Both are valid; the choice depends on whether you value continuity or a clean slate.

### 🧩 Step 5 — Pin & Personalize
Pin Brave to your taskbar so it's one click away. Set it as your default browser if you want external links to open inside its shield. Customize your theme, your search engine, and your home tab to match your daily rhythm.

## 🧠 Setup Guide: Getting the Most Out of Brave

1. **Enable Strict Shields** on sites you trust to load fully, and Aggressive Shields on ad-heavy portals.
2. **Turn on Memory Saver** for laptops that frequently run on battery.
3. **Configure a custom search engine** that matches your privacy preferences.
4. **Set up Sync** only if you own more than one device — and review what's synced periodically.
5. **Review your extension list** quarterly. Fewer extensions mean less attack surface and faster startup.
6. **Enable Automatic HTTPS Upgrades** for sites that don't enforce secure connections by themselves.
7. **Use Tab Groups** to separate work, research, and leisure browsing.
